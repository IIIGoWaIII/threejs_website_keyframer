This page is the viewer output

Everything you scrolled through above is produced by ModelStory, not by this HTML file. The library owns the WebGL canvas, the camera framing, the keyframe choreography and the author-mode gizmo. The demo only supplies a scroll container, a canvas element, a GLB, and a keyframes JSON.

The overlay captions and progress dots you saw are driven purely by the onProgress callback — a few lines of vanilla JS toggling CSS classes. Swap the captions for your own product copy and the same page works for anything: trucks, sneakers, phones, machinery.

Want to re-author the moves? Click "Author mode" above (adds ?edit=1), scroll to a position, move the model or lights with the gizmo, press K to export JSON, and save it as your own positions file.
